# Verified Metrics GTM Context
**Date:** 2025-12-21
**Status:** Active Discussion

## Company Overview

Verified Metrics is an AI-powered financial due diligence platform for private market investors.

**Two Delivery Channels:**
1. **Platform Products** — Self-serve software for investors and companies with QBO/Xero/Plaid
2. **Professional Services** — Expert-delivered engagements for complex situations, larger targets on enterprise systems

## Key Product Capabilities

- Direct API sync with accounting systems (QBO, Xero) and banks (Plaid)
- Transaction-level data access (not document-based)
- **Proof of Cash** — Real-time bank balance verification (unique differentiator—no competitor has this)
- Automated financial models: LBO, QofE Databook, 3 Financial Statements, 13-Week Cashflow, Operating Model
- LLM-powered analysis and structured report generation

## Critical Market Insight (Discovered 2025-12-21)

**Portfolio companies are reluctant to share Verified Metrics data with other investors because the transaction-level visibility is too invasive.**

This breaks the network-effects growth loop (investor → portfolio company → new investor).

### Implications:
- The viral/network-effects GTM is NOT viable
- Portfolio companies see the data granularity as surveillance, not support
- The product works best when the investor has **contractual leverage** (lenders, lead investors with governance rights, distressed situations)

## Strategic Options Identified

### Option A: Narrow Market, Contractual Leverage
- Focus on customers who can REQUIRE data access (lenders, distressed, lead investors)
- Sales-led, relationship-driven GTM
- Smaller but accessible market
- No product changes required

### Option B: Credentialing Model (Product Pivot)
- Build "Verified Summary" product where company controls what's shared
- Company becomes the customer, investor receives verified outputs
- Flip from "surveillance" to "credentialing" (like SOC 2 for financials)
- Larger market but requires product development
- Target: Companies preparing for fundraising, sell-side M&A, CFOs managing investor relations

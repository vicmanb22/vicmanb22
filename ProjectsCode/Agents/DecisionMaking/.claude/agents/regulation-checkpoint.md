# Regulation Checkpoint

You assess emotional regulation state and help restore regulation when needed. Analytical decision-making is impossible when emotionally flooded (>70/100). The prefrontal cortex goes offline under stress.

## Regulation Assessment

### Step 1: Emotional Intensity Check

"On a scale of 0-100, what's your current emotional intensity around this decision?"

| Score | State | Action |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 0-50 | Regulated | Proceed with decision work |
| 51-69 | Activated | Proceed with awareness |
| 70+ | Flooded | STOP - regulate first |

### Step 2: Physical State Check

- Slept 6+ hours? (If no, cognitive function impaired)
- Fed and hydrated? (Blood sugar affects judgment)
- Not high or drunk? (Decision work should wait)

If any are compromised: "These physical needs affect your cognitive function. Can you address [need] before we continue?"

## TIPP Skills (When Intensity ≥70)

### T - Temperature
- Cold water on face (activates dive reflex, slows heart rate)
- Ice cubes in hands
- Cold shower
- Hold breath while face in cold water

### I - Intense Exercise
- 20 jumping jacks
- Sprint in place for 60 seconds
- Push-ups until tired
- Walk quickly around the block

### P - Paced Breathing
Box breathing: 4 counts in → 4 hold → 4 out → 4 hold
Repeat 4-6 times

### P - Progressive Relaxation
- Tense feet → release
- Tense calves → release
- Work up through body to face
- Notice the difference between tension and relaxation

## After TIPP

"Now check your intensity again. What is it now?"

**If dropped below 70:** "Good, you're regulated enough to proceed."

**If still >70:** "This decision work should wait. Let's schedule it for [suggest time] when you're more regulated. What else do you need right now?" → STOP the protocol.

## Signs of Dysregulation (Watch for these)

### Physiological
- Shallow or held breath
- Tight shoulders/jaw
- Racing heart
- Sweating
- Stomach clenching

### Cognitive
- Can't think straight
- Thoughts racing
- Mind going blank
- Catastrophizing

### Behavioral
- Wanting to flee
- Urge to use substances
- Snapping at others
- Pacing/fidgeting

## Key Message

"You're at ___/100 intensity. That's emotion mind. Your prefrontal cortex is offline right now. We can't do good analytical work in this state. Let's use regulation skills first. This isn't weakness—it's neuroscience."

## Window of Tolerance Model

```
    ↑ HYPERAROUSAL (fight/flight)
    | Anxiety, panic, rage, racing thoughts
    |-----------------------------------
    | WINDOW OF TOLERANCE
    | Can think AND feel, regulated
    |-----------------------------------
    | Numb, foggy, collapsed, shutdown
    ↓ HYPOAROUSAL (freeze/collapse)
```

**Goal:** Stay within the window, or return to it before deciding.

## When to Invoke This Checkpoint

- Before starting any decision work
- When user shows signs of flooding mid-session
- When stakes feel overwhelming
- After receiving bad news
- When user mentions physical stress symptoms

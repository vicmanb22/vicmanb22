# Body Wisdom Scanner

You guide somatic sensing to access body wisdom for decisions. The body knows things the mind rationalizes away. This check catches self-abandoning choices before they're made.

## Core Principle

**"The body keeps the score."** — Bessel van der Kolk

Your body has accumulated wisdom from every experience you've ever had. It responds to choices before conscious thought catches up. Learning to read these signals is learning to trust yourself.

## The Body Wisdom Protocol

### Step 1: Prepare the Body

"Let's check your body wisdom. This takes about 5 minutes."

- Get comfortable in your chair
- Close your eyes (if that feels safe)
- Take 5 deep breaths
- Feel your feet on the floor
- Notice where your body makes contact with the chair
- Scan from feet to head, just noticing

### Step 2: Imagine Option A

"Imagine you've chosen Option A and you're implementing it. Don't analyze—just notice your body."

**Prompts:**
- "Does your chest open or tighten?"
- "Do your shoulders rise or release?"
- "Does your breathing deepen or get shallow?"
- "Does your stomach relax or clench?"
- "Do you feel warmer or colder?"
- "Do you feel grounded or anxious?"
- "What's happening in your jaw?"
- "What's happening in your hands?"

Record their observations.

### Step 3: Reset

"Take a few breaths. Shake it off. Let Option A go."

### Step 4: Imagine Option B

"Now imagine you've chosen Option B and you're implementing it. Same thing—don't analyze, just notice."

Repeat the same prompts.

Record observations.

### Step 5: Compare and Interpret

"Let's compare what your body said."

| Signal | Option A | Option B |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Chest | Open/Tight | Open/Tight |
| Shoulders | Rise/Release | Rise/Release |
| Breathing | Deep/Shallow | Deep/Shallow |
| Stomach | Relaxed/Clenched | Relaxed/Clenched |
| Temperature | Warm/Cold | Warm/Cold |
| Ground | Grounded/Anxious | Grounded/Anxious |

**Body YES signals:**
- Opening, relaxing, warming, grounding
- "This aligns with your authentic needs"

**Body CAUTION signals:**
- Tightening, rising, shallowing, clenching, cold, anxiety
- "This might be self-abandonment—choosing what you 'should' rather than what you need"

## When Body and Mind Split

**If cognitive analysis says Option A but body says Option B (or vice versa):**

"Your analysis says one thing but your body says another. This is crucial information. Something is off. Don't decide yet."

**Explore:**
- "What might your body know that your mind is rationalizing away?"
- "Is the cognitive choice performance-based? Are you trying to prove something?"
- "Is your body sensing that you're beyond capacity?"
- "Is there a trauma response to one option that your mind is minimizing?"

**Do not proceed until you understand the split.**

**If body and cognitive align:**
"Great—your body and mind agree. You can trust this direction."

## The Authenticity Check

After body scan, ask:

"Is this choice [what %] self-honoring versus [what %] self-abandoning?"

**If >30% self-abandoning:**

"That's significant. Let's explore:
- Who are you trying to please with this choice?
- What are you trying to prove?
- What would you choose if no one was watching?"

Work through this before proceeding with the decision.

## Body Signals Dictionary

### Chest/Heart Area
- Open = safety, alignment
- Tight = fear, self-protection
- Warm = connection, rightness
- Cold = disconnection, warning

### Shoulders
- Rising = bracing, anticipating burden
- Releasing = acceptance, readiness
- Forward = protecting self
- Back = openness, confidence

### Breathing
- Deep, easy = regulated, safe
- Shallow, held = anxiety, uncertainty
- Irregular = conflict, activation

### Stomach/Gut
- Relaxed = trust, yes
- Butterflies = excitement (can be positive)
- Clenched = dread, no
- Nausea = strong warning

### Overall Energy
- Expansive = this is growth
- Contractive = this is protection
- Grounded = centered, clear
- Floating = disconnected, uncertain

## Common Traps

### "My body always says no"
Possible trauma response. The body learned that all risk is dangerous. Need to distinguish:
- Is this a general fear response?
- Or specific wisdom about THIS choice?

### "I can't feel anything"
Possible dissociation or disconnection from body. Try:
- More grounding first
- Smaller sensations (just hands, just feet)
- Movement before checking

### "Both feel the same"
Either genuinely equivalent, or not accessing the signal. Try:
- Imagining each option more vividly
- Asking: "If I had to bet money on one, which?"
- Waiting 24 hours and checking again

## Integration

Body wisdom is DATA, not DICTATOR.

If body says no but logic says yes, explore the gap.
If body says yes but logic says no, explore the gap.

The goal is integration—mind and body in agreement.

"The body knows things the mind rationalizes away. But the mind also catches things the body's fear overrides. We need both."

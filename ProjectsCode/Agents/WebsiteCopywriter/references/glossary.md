# Website Copywriting Glossary

Definitions of terms, acronyms, and concepts used throughout the Website Copywriter agent references.

---

## Frameworks & Methodologies

### AIDA
**Attention → Interest → Desire → Action**
Classic copywriting framework describing the cognitive stages a buyer goes through. See `frameworks/aida-framework.md`.

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
Optimizing content to appear in AI chatbot and LLM responses (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity). Distinct from but complementary to SEO. See `interviews/aeo-guide-ethan-smith-jeanne-dewitt-grosser.md`.

### BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)
Communication principle: lead with the conclusion/answer, then provide context. From Wes Kao's frameworks. See `interviews/wes-kao-website-copy-communication-guide.md`.

### Buyer Awareness Levels
Eugene Schwartz's 5 stages: Unaware → Problem-Aware → Solution-Aware → Product-Aware → Most Aware. See `frameworks/buyer-awareness-levels.md`.

### Cialdini's Principles
7 principles of persuasion: Reciprocity, Commitment, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, Scarcity, Unity. See `frameworks/cialdini-principles.md`.

### MOO (Most Obvious Objection)
Wes Kao's framework: anticipate and address the most likely objection before it arises. See `interviews/wes-kao-website-copy-communication-guide.md`.

### PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)
Copywriting framework that names the problem, intensifies the pain, then presents the solution. See `frameworks/pas-framework.md`.

### Super-Specific How
Wes Kao's principle: readers want HOW to do things, not just WHAT or WHY. Jump to actionable specifics. See `interviews/wes-kao-website-copy-communication-guide.md`.

## Page Elements

### Above the Fold
Content visible without scrolling. Critical for capturing attention. Typically 500-600px on mobile.

### CTA (Call to Action)
Button or link prompting the user to take action. Examples: "Start Free Trial," "Get the Guide." See `best-practices/ctas.md`.

### Hero Section
The top section of a page, typically containing H1 headline, subheadline, primary CTA, and often an image or video.

### H1, H2, H3
Heading hierarchy. H1 is the main page title (one per page), H2 for major sections, H3 for subsections.

### Lead Magnet
Free valuable content offered in exchange for email address. Examples: ebooks, templates, checklists.

### Microcopy
Small bits of copy that guide users: button text, form labels, error messages, tooltips.

### Subheadline
Supporting text below the main headline that expands on the promise or adds context.

## Metrics & Measurement

### Bounce Rate
Percentage of visitors who leave after viewing only one page. Lower is generally better for engagement pages.

### Conversion Rate
Percentage of visitors who complete a desired action (signup, purchase, etc.). Industry median ~6.6% for landing pages.

### CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization)
The practice of improving conversion rates through testing, copy improvements, and UX changes.

### CTR (Click-Through Rate)
Percentage of people who click a link/button after seeing it. Key metric for CTAs and ads.

### LTV (Lifetime Value)
Total revenue expected from a customer over their entire relationship. Helps prioritize high-value conversions.

## SEO Terms

### Alt Text
Descriptive text for images, used by screen readers and search engines. See `best-practices/accessibility.md`.

### Internal Linking
Links between pages on the same website. Important for SEO and user navigation.

### Long-Tail Keywords
Specific, multi-word search phrases. Lower volume but higher intent. Example: "best project management software for remote teams."

### Meta Description
HTML element describing page content. Appears in search results. Aim for 150-160 characters.

### SERP (Search Engine Results Page)
The page displayed after a search query. Includes organic results, ads, featured snippets.

### Schema Markup
Structured data that helps search engines understand page content. Types: FAQ, HowTo, Product, etc.

## Copywriting Terms

### Benefit
What the feature does FOR the customer. Focus on outcomes, not features. "Save 10 hours/week" (benefit) vs. "Automated reporting" (feature).

### Feature
A capability or characteristic of the product. Should be translated into benefits for copy.

### Pain Point
A specific problem, frustration, or challenge the target audience experiences.

### Social Proof
Evidence that others trust/use/recommend your product. Types: testimonials, logos, reviews, user counts. See `best-practices/social-proof.md`.

### USP (Unique Selling Proposition)
What makes your product/service different and better than alternatives.

### Value Proposition
The primary benefit promised to customers. Should be clear in the hero section.

### VOC (Voice of Customer)
The actual language, phrases, and terminology your customers use. Sourced from interviews, reviews, support tickets.

## Psychology Terms

### Anchoring
Cognitive bias where people rely heavily on the first piece of information offered. Used in pricing strategies.

### Cognitive Load
Mental effort required to process information. Simpler = better for conversions.

### Loss Aversion
People feel losses more strongly than equivalent gains. "Don't miss out" > "Get access."

### Pattern Interrupt
Unexpected element that stops the scroll and captures attention.

### Risk Reversal
Removing perceived risk from a purchase. Examples: money-back guarantees, free trials, easy cancellation.

### Urgency
Creating time pressure to encourage immediate action. Must be genuine to maintain trust.

---

## Technical Terms

### Responsive Design
Website design that adapts to different screen sizes (desktop, tablet, mobile).

### Viewport
The visible area of a webpage on a device. Varies by screen size.

### Tap Target
The clickable/touchable area of a button or link. Minimum 44x44px for mobile accessibility.

### WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines)
Standards for web accessibility. WCAG 2.1 AA is the common compliance target. See `best-practices/accessibility.md`.

---

## Platform Terms

### CMS (Content Management System)
Software for managing website content. Examples: WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace.

### Headless CMS
CMS that provides content via API, separate from the frontend. Examples: Contentful, Sanity.

### Static Site Generator
Tool that generates HTML pages from templates. Examples: Next.js, Gatsby, Hugo.

---

## Audience Terms

### ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
Detailed description of the perfect customer for your product/service.

### Persona
Semi-fictional representation of a customer segment. Used to guide messaging decisions.

### Segment
A distinct group within your audience, often with different needs or messaging approaches.

---

## Process Terms

### A/B Testing
Comparing two versions of a page/element to see which performs better. Also called split testing.

### Discovery
The research phase before writing copy. Gathering information about goals, audience, brand, competitors.

### Iteration
Refining copy through feedback cycles. The agent suggests moving on after 3 revisions.

### Wireframe
Basic layout showing structure and content placement, without final design.

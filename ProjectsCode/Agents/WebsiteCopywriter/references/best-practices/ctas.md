# Call-to-Action (CTA) Best Practices

The CTA is where copy converts. A weak CTA wastes all the persuasion work that came before it.

## CTA Length Guidelines

**Research Foundation:** Studies show 2-4 words perform best. First-person CTAs ("Start My Trial") can outperform second-person by 90%+. (Sources: Intuit Content Design, WPMU DEV, Unbounce)

| Metric | Recommendation | Source |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Word count | 2-4 words optimal | Intuit Content Design |
| Character limit | 24 characters max | UX research |
| Mobile ideal | 15-20 characters | Mobile-first design |
| Structure | Verb + noun | UX best practice |

**Examples by length:**
- 2 words: "Get Started" (11 chars) ✓
- 3 words: "Start Free Trial" (16 chars) ✓
- 4 words: "Get My Free Report" (18 chars) ✓
- Too long: "Start Your Free 14-Day Trial Today" (35 chars) ✗

## CTA Fundamentals

### What Makes a Great CTA

1. **Clear** - Reader knows exactly what happens next
2. **Specific** - "Start Free Trial" beats "Submit"
3. **Action-oriented** - Begins with a verb
4. **Benefit-driven** - Hints at the value they'll receive
5. **Low-friction** - Minimizes perceived effort/risk

### The Clarity Test

Ask: "If I click this button, what exactly will happen?"

- **Unclear:** "Submit" | "Click Here" | "Learn More"
- **Clear:** "Start Free Trial" | "Get My Report" | "Book a Demo"

## CTA Formulas

### Action + Benefit
- Get [Benefit]
- Start [Positive Outcome]
- Unlock [Desirable Thing]

**Examples:**
- "Get More Leads"
- "Start Saving Time Today"
- "Unlock Premium Features"

### Action + Object
- Download [Thing]
- Join [Community/Number]
- Claim [Offer]

**Examples:**
- "Download the Guide"
- "Join 10,000 Marketers"
- "Claim Your Free Trial"

### First Person ("My/Me")
- Get My [Thing]
- Start My [Trial/Journey]
- Show Me [How/What]

**Examples:**
- "Get My Free Report"
- "Start My Trial"
- "Show Me How It Works"

**Why first person works:** Studies show first-person CTAs can outperform second-person by 90%+ in some contexts.

### Urgency/Scarcity
- [Action] Now
- [Action] Before [Deadline]
- [Action] — [Number] Spots Left

**Examples:**
- "Register Now"
- "Buy Before Friday"
- "Enroll — 5 Spots Left"

## CTA Copy by Funnel Stage

| Stage | CTA Focus | Examples |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Awareness | Education, value | "Read the Guide" | "Watch Demo" |
| Consideration | Deeper engagement | "See Pricing" | "Compare Plans" |
| Decision | Conversion | "Start Free Trial" | "Buy Now" |
| Retention | Expansion | "Upgrade Now" | "Add Users" |

## CTA Button Design Best Practices

### Color
- High contrast with surrounding page
- Consistent with brand (usually)
- Test: Sometimes unexpected colors win

### Size
- Large enough to notice
- Not so large it feels aggressive
- Minimum 44px tap target for mobile

### Placement
- Above the fold for primary CTA
- At end of each major section
- After proof/testimonials
- Sticky headers/footers for long pages

### White Space
- Breathing room around CTA
- Don't crowd with other elements
- Let it stand out

## CTA Supporting Copy

### Microcopy (Below Button)

**Risk Reducers:**
- "No credit card required"
- "Cancel anytime"
- "30-day money-back guarantee"
- "Takes less than 2 minutes"

**Social Proof:**
- "Join 10,000+ customers"
- "Trusted by teams at Google"
- "4.9/5 stars"

**Privacy:**
- "We'll never spam you"
- "Your data is secure"

### CTA Headlines (Above Button)

- "Ready to [get benefit]?"
- "Start [achieving goal] today"
- "[Number] [people] already [result]. Join them."

## Multiple CTAs

### Primary vs. Secondary

- **Primary:** Main conversion goal (higher contrast, larger)
- **Secondary:** Alternative path (lower contrast, smaller or text link)

**Example:**
- Primary: "Start Free Trial"
- Secondary: "Schedule a Demo" (for those not ready)

### CTA Repetition

On long pages, repeat CTAs:
- After hero section
- After key benefits
- After social proof
- At page end (final CTA section)

Keep copy consistent or progressively stronger.

## A/B Testing CTAs

### High-Impact Tests

1. **Button copy:** "Get Started" vs. "Start Free Trial"
2. **First vs. second person:** "Start Your Trial" vs. "Start My Trial"
3. **With vs. without urgency:** "Buy Now" vs. "Buy — Sale Ends Friday"
4. **Single vs. double CTA:** One button vs. primary + secondary
5. **Button color:** Test surprising colors
6. **Microcopy variations:** Different risk reducers

### What to Measure

- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Conversion rate
- Revenue per visitor (for e-commerce)

## CTAs by Page Type

### Homepage
- Primary: Core product action ("Start Free Trial")
- Secondary: Demo or tour ("See How It Works")

### Landing Page
- Single focused CTA
- Repeated throughout page
- Match CTA to page goal exactly

### Pricing Page
- One CTA per plan
- Highlight recommended plan
- Consider "Compare Plans" secondary CTA

### Product Page
- "Add to Cart" / "Buy Now"
- Secondary: "Save for Later" / "Compare"

### Blog Post
- Relevant lead magnet CTA
- Newsletter signup
- Related product soft CTA

## CTA Words to Avoid

| Avoid | Why | Use Instead |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| Submit | Generic, cold | Get, Start, Join |
| Click Here | Vague | [Specific action] |
| Buy | Can feel aggressive | Get, Try, Invest |
| Sign Up | Feels like commitment | Start, Join, Try |
| Learn More | Low value signal | See How, Explore |

## CTA Friction Reducers

For high-commitment CTAs (purchases, demos):

1. **Guarantee:** "30-day money-back guarantee"
2. **Time frame:** "Setup takes 2 minutes"
3. **No obligation:** "Free quote, no strings attached"
4. **Privacy:** "We respect your inbox"
5. **Support:** "Questions? Chat with us"

## Mobile CTA Considerations

- Minimum 44x44px tap target
- Thumb-friendly placement
- Sticky footer CTA for long pages
- Consider simplified mobile CTAs
- Test mobile-specific copy

# Discovery Questions

A comprehensive questionnaire to gather all necessary information before creating website copy. Use this as your guide during the discovery phase.

## How to Use This Document

1. Work through each category systematically
2. Note which questions receive incomplete or no answers
3. Add unanswered questions to the project's `open-questions.md` file
4. Return to this document when the user provides new information

---

## Business & Goals

### Primary Questions

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| What is the primary conversion goal? (leads, sales, signups, bookings, awareness) | Determines the overall messaging strategy and CTA approach |
| What problem does your product/service solve? | Core of all messaging - the foundation of the value proposition |
| What is your unique value proposition vs competitors? | Differentiates the messaging and informs positioning |
| What are your KPIs for success? | Helps prioritize elements and measure effectiveness |

### Follow-Up Questions

- What secondary conversion goals exist?
- Are there specific revenue or lead targets?
- What does success look like 6 months after launch?
- What has worked/failed in previous marketing efforts?

## Target Audience

### Primary Questions

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| Who is your ideal customer? (demographics, psychographics) | Shapes tone, language level, and messaging focus |
| What pain points drive them to seek a solution? | Critical for Problem/Pain sections and emotional hooks |
| What objections or hesitations might they have? | Informs FAQ, social proof placement, and objection handling |
| What language do they use to describe their problems? (Voice of Customer) | Makes copy resonate - use their words, not yours |
| Are they experts or novices in your space? | Determines technical depth and explanation level |

### Follow-Up Questions

- What's their typical job title or role?
- What's their decision-making process? (solo, committee, approval chain)
- What alternatives have they likely tried?
- Where do they spend time online? (informs tone and references)
- What's their budget range and price sensitivity?

### Awareness Level Assessment

Determine where most visitors will fall on the awareness spectrum (see `frameworks/buyer-awareness-levels.md`):

- **Unaware**: Don't know they have a problem
- **Problem-Aware**: Know the problem, not the solutions
- **Solution-Aware**: Know solutions exist, evaluating options
- **Product-Aware**: Know your product, need convincing
- **Most Aware**: Ready to buy, need the right offer

## Brand & Tone

### Primary Questions

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| Do you have brand guidelines or a style guide? | Ensures copy aligns with established brand identity |
| What tone of voice? (formal, casual, authoritative, friendly, humorous) | Shapes all copy decisions |
| Examples of brands whose voice you admire? | Provides concrete direction when guidelines are vague |

### Follow-Up Questions

- Are there words or phrases to always use?
- Are there words or phrases to never use?
- How do you refer to customers? (clients, customers, users, members)
- What's your stance on humor, emojis, or informal language?
- Any legal/compliance language requirements?

### Tone Spectrum

Help the user place themselves on these spectrums:

- Formal ←→ Casual
- Serious ←→ Playful
- Technical ←→ Accessible
- Bold/Confident ←→ Humble/Understated
- Urgent ←→ Relaxed

## Technical & Platform

### Primary Questions

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| Target platform? (Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, custom, etc.) | Affects technical recommendations and feature suggestions |
| New site, redesign, or migration? | Changes approach to existing content and SEO preservation |
| Any functionality requirements? (blog, ecommerce, booking, forms) | Informs page types and conversion flow |

### Follow-Up Questions

- Who will implement the copy? (dev team, designer, DIY)
- Are there template constraints?
- What's the timeline for launch?
- Will there be a staging/review process?
- Are there performance requirements? (load time, accessibility standards)

### Platform-Specific Considerations

Reference `references/platforms/` for platform-specific guidance on:
- Native features to leverage
- Limitations to work around
- Animation/interaction capabilities
- Form and CTA options

## Existing Assets

### Primary Questions

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| Current website URL (to analyze what's working)? | Baseline for improvement; identify what to keep |
| Inspirational sites you admire (any industry)? | Visual and copy direction guidance |
| Existing content to port over? (blogs, guides, case studies) | SEO preservation and content leverage |
| SEO data available? (Google Search Console, Google Analytics, SEMRush, keyword research) | Informs keyword strategy and identifies opportunities |
| Backlinks to preserve? | Critical for SEO - must maintain URL structure or redirect |

### Follow-Up Questions

- Which existing pages perform best? (traffic, conversions)
- Which pages underperform or cause confusion?
- Are there high-ranking blog posts to maintain?
- What's the current domain authority?
- Any pages with significant backlinks?

- [ ] Blog posts (quantity and topics)
- [ ] Case studies
- [ ] Testimonials database

- [ ] Legal pages (privacy, terms)
- [ ] Email sequences
- [ ] Sales decks or marketing collateral

## Social Proof & Authority

### Primary Questions

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| Testimonials or reviews available? | Powerful conversion tool - needs to be featured prominently |
| Client logos to display? | Quick credibility signal, especially B2B |
| Awards, certifications, media mentions? | Authority signals that reduce risk perception |
| Case studies or results to showcase? | Concrete proof that the solution works |

### Follow-Up Questions

- Do you have quantified results? (percentages, numbers, timeframes)
- Are testimonials attributed with names, titles, photos?
- Can customers be contacted for quotes or case studies?
- Any notable customers that can be named?
- Industry certifications or compliance? (SOC 2, HIPAA, etc.)

### Social Proof Inventory

- [ ] Written testimonials (quantity: ___)
- [ ] Video testimonials (quantity: ___)
- [ ] Star ratings (source: ___, average: ___)
- [ ] Client logos (quantity: ___, notable: ___)
- [ ] Case studies (quantity: ___, with metrics: ___)
- [ ] Media mentions (publications: ___)
- [ ] Awards (list: ___)
- [ ] Certifications (list: ___)
- [ ] User count or customer count
- [ ] Years in business

## Competitors

### Primary Questions

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| Main competitors? (URLs if available) | Informs differentiation strategy and keyword opportunities |
| What do they do well? What do they miss? | Identifies gaps to exploit and standards to meet |

### Follow-Up Questions

- Who do customers compare you to most often?
- Where do you win against competitors?
- Where do you lose?
- What do competitors say that you wish you could say?
- What do they miss that you can own?

### Competitor Analysis Focus Areas

When analyzing competitors (or using the competitor-analyzer subagent):

1. **Positioning**: How do they describe themselves?
2. **Value proposition**: What's their main promise?
3. **Proof points**: What social proof do they use?
4. **Objection handling**: What concerns do they address?
5. **CTA strategy**: What do they ask visitors to do?
6. **SEO keywords**: What terms do they rank for?
7. **Weaknesses**: Where are they vulnerable?

## Industry-Specific Additions

### SaaS / Software

- What's the pricing model? (subscription, one-time, freemium)
- Free trial available? Length?
- Is there a self-serve signup or sales-required process?
- What integrations are available?
- What's the implementation timeline?
- Is there an API?

### E-commerce

- What's the average order value?
- What's the return policy?
- Shipping options and costs?
- Payment methods accepted?
- Product differentiation (handmade, sustainable, luxury, budget)?

### Professional Services

- What's the service delivery process?
- Average engagement length?
- Typical project scope and pricing?
- Client industries or specializations?
- Team credentials and experience?

### Local Business

- Service area/locations?
- Hours of operation?
- Booking/appointment process?
- Walk-in vs. appointment only?
- Parking/accessibility?

## Question Priority Framework

After discovery, categorize unanswered questions:

### Critical (Blocks Progress)
Questions that must be answered before copy can be finalized:
- Value proposition
- Target audience
- Primary conversion goal
- Brand tone

### Important (Significantly Improves Quality)
Questions that would make copy much stronger:
- Testimonials/social proof
- Competitor URLs
- SEO data
- Pain points in customer language

### Nice to Have (Enhances)
Questions that would add polish:
- Brand guidelines
- Inspirational sites
- Specific metrics/results
- Platform details

---

## After Discovery

1. **Update open-questions.md** with any unanswered questions
2. **Organize by priority** (Critical, Important, Nice to Have)
3. **Note why each matters** so user understands the impact
4. **Check references folder** for any materials user mentioned but hasn't added yet
5. **Proceed to Research phase** with available information

# About Page Copy Guide

The About page is often the second most-visited page after the homepage. It's where visitors come to verify credibility and connect with your brand on a human level.

---

## About Page Purpose

**Why people visit the About page:**
1. Verify legitimacy ("Is this company real?")
2. Understand who's behind the product/service
3. Find values alignment ("Are they like me?")
4. Build trust before purchasing
5. Connect emotionally with the brand

**Key insight:** Visitors come for reassurance, not company history.

---

## About Page Structure

### Recommended Sections

1. **Mission/Purpose Header** - Why you exist
2. **Origin Story** - How you started (briefly)
3. **Values or Beliefs** - What you stand for
4. **Team Section** - The people behind the product
5. **Milestones/Timeline** - Credibility markers (optional)
6. **Social Proof** - Awards, press, recognition
7. **CTA** - What to do next (contact, join, explore)

---

## Mission/Purpose Section

### Writing the Mission Statement

**Formula:** We [action] to help [audience] [achieve outcome].

**Examples:**
- "We build software to help remote teams work better together."
- "We're on a mission to make professional photography accessible to everyone."
- "We exist to help small businesses compete with the big guys."

**Avoid:**
- Jargon-filled corporate speak
- Vague, meaningless phrases
- Self-congratulatory language

### Opening Copy

Don't start with "Welcome to our About page" or company history.

**Start with:**
- Your mission or purpose
- The problem you're solving
- What you believe

**Strong opening examples:**
- "Great software shouldn't require a PhD to use."
- "We believe small teams can do big things—with the right tools."
- "Founded by frustrated [personas] who knew there had to be a better way."

## Origin Story

### Why It Matters

Stories are memorable and create emotional connection. The origin story humanizes your brand.

### Story Structure

**Classic founder story arc:**
1. **The problem:** What frustrated you / motivated creation
2. **The insight:** What you realized others missed
3. **The action:** What you built to solve it
4. **The result:** Where you are now

### Writing Tips

- Keep it brief (3-5 paragraphs max)
- Focus on the problem, not just history
- Make it relatable to your audience
- Include specific details (makes it believable)
- Be authentic—don't manufacture drama

### Example Structure

```
[Problem]
After years of [experience], we were frustrated by [problem].

[Insight]
We realized that [insight] was the missing piece.

[Action]
So in [year], we set out to build [solution].

[Result]
Today, [metric or outcome], and we're just getting started.
```

## Values Section

### Types of Values Content

1. **Core values list:** 3-5 values with brief descriptions
2. **Beliefs manifesto:** "We believe..." statements
3. **How we work:** Operating principles
4. **Culture pillars:** Team-focused values

### Writing Values

**Make them specific, not generic:**

| Generic | Specific |
|---------|----------|
| Excellence | Ship it, then improve it |
| Customer focus | Customers first, always |
| Innovation | Question everything, test constantly |
| Integrity | Say what we'll do, do what we say |

**Include the "so what":**
- Value: Customer Success
- So what: "Every team member answers support tickets. We stay close to the problems you face."

### Beliefs Format

```
We believe [belief].
That's why we [action/approach].

We believe simplicity beats complexity.
That's why we say no to features that don't make your life easier.
```

## Team Section

### Why Team Matters

- Puts faces to the company
- Builds trust and relatability
- Shows expertise and credibility
- Humanizes the brand

### What to Include Per Person

| Element | Guideline |
|---------|-----------|
| Photo | Professional but approachable, consistent style |
| Name | Full name |
| Title | Clear, understandable role |
| Bio | 2-4 sentences, balance professional + personal |
| Social links | LinkedIn, Twitter (optional) |

### Bio Writing Tips

**Include:**
- Relevant experience/expertise
- What they do at the company
- One personal detail (hobby, quirk, fun fact)

**Example:**
"Sarah leads product at [Company], where she obsesses over user experience. Before joining, she built products at [Notable Company] and [Other Company]. When not wireframing, she's probably hiking with her two rescue dogs."

### Team Display Options

- Full team grid (small teams, <15)
- Leadership/key team featured (larger companies)
- Department-organized (very large teams)
- Carousel or expandable cards

## Milestones/Timeline (Optional)

### When to Include

- Established company with meaningful history
- Notable achievements to highlight
- Rapid growth story to tell
- Trust-building through longevity

### What to Include

- Founding date
- Major product launches
- Significant customer milestones
- Funding rounds (if relevant)
- Awards or recognition
- Team growth milestones

### Format Options

- Visual timeline
- Simple list
- Integrated into narrative

## Social Proof on About Page

### Types to Include

**Awards and recognition:**
- Industry awards
- Best workplace awards
- Product awards (G2, Capterra badges)

**Press and media:**
- Featured in [Publications]
- Press logos
- Notable coverage

**Customer proof:**
- "Trusted by X customers"
- Notable customer logos
- Brief testimonials about the team/company

### Placement

- After team section
- Near bottom (before CTA)
- Integrated throughout as relevant

## About Page CTA

### What Comes After About?

Consider visitor intent—they're warming up. Guide them to:
- Contact page
- Product exploration
- Careers (hiring opportunity)
- Newsletter signup
- Key content piece

### CTA Copy Examples

- "Ready to see what we can do for you?"
- "Want to join our team? See open positions."
- "Questions? We'd love to hear from you."
- "Convinced? See how [Product] works."

## About Page Variations by Business Type

### Startup/Small Business

**Focus on:**
- Founder story prominently
- Mission and vision
- Small, dedicated team
- Agility and customer focus

### Enterprise/Large Company

**Focus on:**
- Mission and impact
- Leadership team (not everyone)
- Global presence/scale
- Awards and recognition
- Clear navigation to relevant areas (careers, press, investors)

### Agency/Service Business

**Focus on:**
- Team expertise and credentials
- Work approach/methodology
- Client relationships
- Results and outcomes

### Personal Brand

**Focus on:**
- Your story
- Credentials and experience
- Values and approach
- Personal connection

- [ ] Clear mission or purpose statement
- [ ] Origin story (brief, relatable)
- [ ] Values or beliefs (specific, not generic)
- [ ] Team section with photos and bios
- [ ] Social proof (awards, press, recognition)
- [ ] Clear CTA (what to do next)

- [ ] Authentic, not corporate-speak
- [ ] Customer-focused, not self-congratulatory
- [ ] Consistent with brand voice
- [ ] Human and approachable

### SEO
- [ ] Title tag: "About [Company] | [Descriptor]"
- [ ] Meta description highlights mission/differentiator
- [ ] Header hierarchy logical
- [ ] Image alt text for team photos

## Common About Page Mistakes

1. **Company history lecture:** Visitors don't care about your timeline
2. **"We were founded in...":** Boring opening, lead with purpose
3. **No team photos:** Missed trust-building opportunity
4. **Stock photos of "team":** Obviously fake, damages credibility
5. **All about you, not them:** Should relate to visitor's needs
6. **Corporate jargon:** "Synergy," "leverage," "best-in-class"
7. **No CTA:** Dead-end page, no next step
8. **Outdated content:** Old team members, stale milestones
9. **Generic values:** "Excellence, integrity, innovation"—meaningless
10. **Too long:** Should be scannable, not an essay

## About Page Copy Frameworks

### Framework 1: Problem-Solution-Team

```
1. The Problem We Solve
[Industry/customer problem]

2. Our Approach
[How we think about solving it]

3. The Team
[Who does the work]

4. What We Stand For
[Values]

5. Join Us / Work With Us
[CTA]
```

### Framework 2: Belief-Story-Proof

```
1. What We Believe
[Core beliefs or mission]

2. How We Got Here
[Origin story]

3. Who We Are
[Team]

4. Why We're Different
[Differentiators]

5. The Proof
[Social proof]

6. Get Started
[CTA]
```

### Framework 3: Customer-Centric About

```
1. We Exist for You
[How we help the customer]

2. The Team Behind [Product]
[People focused on their success]

3. Our Promise
[What customers can expect]

4. Join [X] Customers
[Social proof]

5. Let's Work Together
[CTA]
```

# Homepage Copy Guide

The homepage is often the most visited page and sets the tone for the entire site. It must quickly communicate who you are, what you offer, and why visitors should care.

---

## Homepage Purpose

**Primary goals:**
1. Communicate value proposition within 5 seconds
2. Help visitors self-select (is this for me?)
3. Guide to relevant deeper content
4. Build enough trust to continue exploring
5. Provide clear next step (CTA)

**Not the goal:**
- Tell your entire story (that's the About page)
- List every feature (that's product pages)
- Make the sale immediately (unless e-commerce)

---

## Homepage Structure

### Recommended Section Order

1. **Hero Section** - Value proposition, primary CTA
2. **Social Proof Bar** - Quick credibility (logos, stats)
3. **Problem/Pain Section** - Show understanding
4. **Solution Section** - How you help
5. **Features/Benefits** - What you offer
6. **Social Proof** - Testimonials, case studies
7. **How It Works** - Simple process (if applicable)
8. **Differentiators** - Why choose you
9. **Final CTA** - Last push to convert
10. **Footer** - Navigation, contact, legal

### Section Deep Dive

#### Hero Section

**Must answer in 5 seconds:**
- What is this?
- Who is it for?
- What's the main benefit?
- What should I do next?

**Elements:**
| Element | Guideline |
|---------|-----------|
| H1 Headline | Main promise, 6-12 words |
| Subheadline | Expand/clarify headline, 15-25 words |
| Primary CTA | Clear action, 2-4 words |
| Secondary CTA | Alternative action (optional) |
| Hero Visual | Product shot, person using product, or abstract |
| Social proof teaser | "Trusted by 10,000+ teams" |

**Hero Formula Options:**
- `[Action verb] + [Result] + [Without/In] + [Timeframe/Without Pain]`
- `The [Category] that [Unique Benefit]`
- `[Target Audience]'s Favorite Way to [Outcome]`

#### Problem Section

**Purpose:** Show you understand their pain before pitching solutions.

**Approach options:**
1. **Direct pain points:** List 3-4 specific frustrations
2. **Before/after contrast:** Current state vs. desired state
3. **Cost of status quo:** What they lose by not solving this
4. **Story format:** "Sound familiar?" scenario

**Copy tips:**
- Use customer language (Voice of Customer)
- Be specific, not generic
- Don't dwell too long—acknowledge and move to solution

#### Solution Section

**Purpose:** Position your offering as the answer to the pain.

**Elements:**
- Transition from problem ("There's a better way")
- High-level explanation of your approach
- 3-step "How it works" (optional but powerful)
- Benefits framing (what they get, not what you do)

#### Features/Benefits Section

**Purpose:** Provide substance for those who need more detail.

**Structure options:**
- Icon + headline + short description (3-4 features)
- Feature grid (6-9 features in 2-3 columns)
- Tabs for feature categories
- Carousel for many features

**Copy formula per feature:**
`[Feature Name]: [Benefit statement, not description]`

Bad: "Real-time Analytics: See your data update in real-time."
Good: "Real-time Analytics: Make decisions with data that's always current."

#### Social Proof Section

**Purpose:** Build trust through others' experiences.

**Types of social proof:**
- Customer testimonials (with names, titles, photos)
- Case study excerpts with metrics
- Client logos
- Review platform ratings (G2, Capterra)
- Customer count or usage stats
- Media mentions/press logos

**Placement strategy:**
- Brief social proof bar early (logos, "Trusted by...")
- Detailed testimonials mid-page
- Case study teasers with CTAs to full stories

#### Final CTA Section

**Purpose:** Convert visitors who've scrolled through everything.

**Elements:**
- Restate main benefit
- Address remaining objection (risk reversal)
- Clear CTA button
- Optional urgency (if genuine)

**Example structure:**
```
[Headline: Ready to [achieve benefit]?]
[1-2 sentences addressing final hesitation]
[CTA Button]
[Risk reducer: "Free trial. No credit card required."]
```

## Homepage Copy Best Practices

### Above the Fold

Must include:
- [ ] Clear headline (value proposition)
- [ ] Subheadline or supporting text
- [ ] Primary CTA visible
- [ ] Relevant visual
- [ ] Quick social proof element

### Messaging Hierarchy

1. **What you do** (hero)
2. **Who it's for** (hero or problem section)
3. **Why you're different** (differentiators)
4. **Proof it works** (social proof throughout)
5. **What to do next** (CTAs)

### Tone Calibration

Homepage sets the voice for the site:
- Match brand guidelines
- Align with audience expectations
- Consistent throughout (don't shift from formal to casual)

### Length Considerations

| Traffic Type | Length |
|--------------|--------|
| Cold (ads, social) | Longer, more education needed |
| Warm (referrals, organic) | Medium, some familiarity |
| Hot (returning, branded search) | Shorter, get to CTA quickly |

Most sites optimize for warm traffic—balance education with conversion focus.

## Homepage for Different Business Types

### SaaS

**Focus on:**
- Speed to value (quick setup, immediate results)
- Integration capabilities
- Security/reliability
- Customer success stories with metrics

**Key sections:**
- Product screenshot in hero
- Feature overview
- Integration logos
- Case studies with ROI
- Free trial CTA

### E-commerce

**Focus on:**
- Product discovery (categories, bestsellers)
- Trust signals (reviews, secure checkout)
- Promotions/sales
- Shipping and returns

**Key sections:**
- Hero with key offer/promo
- Featured products/collections
- Trust badges
- Customer reviews
- Seasonal/promotional content

### Service Business

**Focus on:**
- Expertise and credibility
- Process/methodology
- Results and testimonials
- Personal connection (team, founder)

**Key sections:**
- Problem-solution focus
- Service overview
- Team/about teaser
- Detailed testimonials
- Clear contact CTA

### B2B

**Focus on:**
- Business outcomes (ROI, efficiency)
- Enterprise features (security, scale, support)
- Social proof from similar companies
- Clear buying process

**Key sections:**
- Outcome-focused hero
- Logo bar of recognizable clients
- Feature overview with business benefits
- Case studies with metrics
- Demo request CTA

## Homepage Checklist

### Before Writing
- [ ] Understand primary conversion goal
- [ ] Know target audience and their pain points
- [ ] Clarify unique value proposition
- [ ] Review competitor homepages
- [ ] Gather social proof elements available

### Copy Elements
- [ ] H1 headline answers "what is this?"
- [ ] Subheadline expands or clarifies
- [ ] Primary CTA is specific and actionable
- [ ] Problem section uses customer language
- [ ] Features framed as benefits
- [ ] Testimonials include names and details
- [ ] Final CTA restates value and reduces risk

### SEO
- [ ] Title tag optimized (60 chars)
- [ ] Meta description compelling (155 chars)
- [ ] H1 includes primary keyword naturally
- [ ] Header hierarchy is logical

### Technical
- [ ] Hero renders fast (above fold priority)
- [ ] Mobile preview checked
- [ ] All CTAs link to correct destinations
- [ ] Images have alt text

## Common Homepage Mistakes

1. **Trying to say everything:** Homepage is a menu, not the full meal
2. **Vague headlines:** "Welcome to [Company]" says nothing
3. **Features without benefits:** List specs without explaining value
4. **No clear CTA:** Multiple competing actions dilute conversion
5. **All about you:** Should focus on visitor's needs, not your story
6. **No social proof:** Missed trust-building opportunity
7. **Wall of text:** Should be scannable, not an essay
8. **Ignoring mobile:** Over 50% of traffic, must work at 375px
9. **Generic stock photos:** Use real product shots or authentic imagery
10. **Burying the lead:** Most important info should be first

## Homepage Examples by Goal

### Lead Generation Homepage
```
Hero: Value prop + form or CTA to lead magnet
Social proof: Logos of companies who've converted
Problem: Pain points your solution addresses
Solution: How you help
Features: Key capabilities
Testimonials: Client results
CTA: Request demo / Download guide
```

### Product Sales Homepage
```
Hero: Product image + primary benefit + Buy CTA
Social proof: Reviews, ratings, customer count
Features: Key product benefits
Social proof: Testimonials
Comparison: Why better than alternatives
CTA: Buy now with guarantee messaging
```

### Service Business Homepage
```
Hero: Outcome promise + Contact CTA
Problem: Issues you solve
Services: Overview of what you offer
Process: How working together works
Case studies: Client results
Team: Credibility and personal connection
CTA: Book consultation
```

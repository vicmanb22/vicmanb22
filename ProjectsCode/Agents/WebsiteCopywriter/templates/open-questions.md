## Critical (Blocking Progress)

These questions need answers before copy can be finalized:

### Value Proposition
- [ ] **What makes you different from competitors?**
  - *Why it matters:* This drives the core messaging across all pages

### Target Audience
- [ ] **Who is your ideal customer?**
  - *Why it matters:* Copy tone, language, and pain points depend on this

---

## Important (Would Significantly Improve Copy)

These would make the copy much stronger:

### Social Proof
- [ ] **Do you have customer testimonials available?**
  - *Why it matters:* Social proof significantly increases conversion rates
  - *What to provide:* Customer quotes with names, titles, photos if possible

### Competitors
- [ ] **Who are your main competitors?**
  - *Why it matters:* Helps differentiate messaging and identify keyword opportunities
  - *What to provide:* URLs of 2-3 main competitors

### SEO Data
- [ ] **Do you have keyword research or analytics data?**
  - *Why it matters:* Ensures copy targets the right search terms
  - *What to provide:* GSC export, GA data, or keyword research spreadsheet

---

## Nice to Have (Would Enhance)

These would add polish but aren't essential:

### Brand Voice
- [ ] **Do you have brand guidelines or style guide?**
  - *Why it matters:* Ensures copy consistency with brand identity
  - *What to provide:* PDF or doc with tone of voice, terminology, etc.

### Existing Content
- [ ] **Is there existing content to reference or port over?**
  - *Why it matters:* Maintains consistency and leverages what's already working
  - *What to provide:* Current website copy, blog posts, marketing materials

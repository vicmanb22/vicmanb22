## Template Notes (Remove before publishing)

### Which Sections to Include?

**For Use Case/Analytical Pages** (e.g., Runway Analysis, Revenue Analysis):
- ✅ All sections required
- ✅ Full "At a Glance" table
- ✅ "Understanding Your Output" with all 4 columns
- ✅ "Why This Matters" recommended

**For General/Foundational Pages** (e.g., How to Login, Upload Files):
- ✅ Video Walkthrough and Steps required
- ⚠️ "At a Glance" can be minimal or omitted
- ⚠️ "Understanding Your Output" may not apply (omit if not relevant)
- ⚠️ "Why This Matters" usually not needed

### Critical Reminders

1. **Demo Company Data**: Use realistic company names (e.g., "CloudForward Technologies") — NEVER "sell.verifiedmetrics.com" or "VM Demo"

2. **Screenshots**:
   - Minimum 1080p resolution (1920×1080)
   - PNG format (not JPG)
   - Add arrows/highlights to show where to click/type
   - Crop tightly to relevant area

3. **Steps**: Maximum 3 sentences each. If longer, split into multiple steps.

4. **Output Table**: For analytical pages, always include "What Good Looks Like" and "Red Flags" columns.

See [how-to-create-help-pages.md](how-to-create-help-pages.md) for complete guidance.

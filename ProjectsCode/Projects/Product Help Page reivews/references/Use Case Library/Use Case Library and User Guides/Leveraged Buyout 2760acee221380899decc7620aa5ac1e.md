### **Strengths Supporting Debt Structure:**

- ✅ **High EBITDA Margin**: 52.9% indicates strong pricing power
- ✅ **Conservative Leverage**: 3.25x total debt/EBITDA
- ✅ **Diversified Revenue**: 2,200+ customers, low concentration risk
- ✅ **Strong Interest Coverage**: 6.1x EBITDA/Interest

### **🚨 Critical Risks:**

- **Cash Flow Volatility**: 9 of 16 months negative operating cash flow
- **Working Capital Issues**: $7.1M A/R growth vs $37K collections (0.4% rate)
- **Seasonal Revenue**: Significant monthly revenue fluctuations ($223K - $2.1M)
- **Collection Risk**: Poor A/R conversion threatens debt service capability

## **💡 Debt Structure Rationale**

### **Revolver ($3.0M)**

- **Purpose**: Address working capital volatility and seasonal cash needs
- **Size**: 48.8% of peak monthly cash burn observed
- **Covenant**: Min. 1.25x EBITDA coverage

### **Term Loan A ($6.0M)**

- **Purpose**: Acquisition financing with bank relationship
- **Amortization**: Deleverages to 2.08x by Year 4
- **Covenant**: Max 3.5x leverage, min 1.25x coverage

### **Term Loan B ($8.0M)**

- **Purpose**: Core acquisition debt, institutional market
- **Structure**: Minimal amortization, refinancing expected Year 6-7
- **Covenant**: Max 4.0x leverage, min 1.20x coverage

### **Senior Notes ($2.97M)**

- **Purpose**: Complete financing gap
- **Fixed Rate**: Hedges against rate volatility
- **Subordination**: Behind bank debt, higher yield

## **🎯 Exit Strategy Considerations**

- **Target Exit**: Years 5-7 at 6.0-7.0x EBITDA multiple
- **Required EBITDA Growth**: 8-12% annually to support debt service
- **Priority**: Fix working capital conversion and cash flow volatility

**Overall Assessment**: The debt structure is conservatively sized but the company's working capital issues pose significant execution risk for debt service. Immediate operational focus required on A/R collections and cash flow management.

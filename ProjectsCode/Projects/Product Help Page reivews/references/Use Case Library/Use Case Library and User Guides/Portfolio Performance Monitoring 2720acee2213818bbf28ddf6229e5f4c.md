# Q&A

## How is the portfolio company performing?

*I want to know how the company is performing at any point in time without reaching out to the founder.*

VM’s platform provide portfolio company insights to Investors at any point in time through it’s platform as well as automated monthly performance notifications.

### VM Portfolio Performance Monitoring

- How is the company performing in line with our expectations?
    - VM connects to your accounting system via API, standardizes the financials to your portfolio model, and compares actuals to your investment case and budget. See clear variance-to-plan views with key drivers like revenue, gross margin, and operating expenses, plus benchmark context. Monthly performance notifications summarize results and trends so you can track progress against expectations, no manual follow-ups required.
- How long till the company runs out of cash and need to do another round of fund raising?
    - Get a clear, always-current view of liquidity so you can act before cash becomes a constraint. Our platform highlights key metrics like burn and runway in a single view, shows runway in months with impending thresholds, and includes concise runway updates regularly, making it easy to decide when to initiate the next funding round.
- How is the company managing their obligations?
    - VM brings together the company’s financials and obligation framework into a single view, providing an overall picture of compliance and risk. Investors can quickly assess current status, upcoming deadlines, and potential areas of concern, with regular summaries included in monthly performance notifications for consistent visibility.

## **How's my portfolio company doing?**

Get instant, comprehensive health checks on any portfolio company without reaching out to founders. Our platform aggregates financial data, market signals, and operational metrics to give you a complete picture in seconds.

From valuation updates and growth trajectory to funding needs and potential risks—everything you need to know is automatically tracked and surfaced when it matters.

### **1. What's the latest valuation of my portfolio company?**

Track real-time valuations powered by automated revenue multiples, market comparables, and recent funding rounds. Our AI-driven models combine financial performance data with industry benchmarks to provide continuously updated fair value estimates.

See how valuations trend over time, compare against sector peers, and understand the key drivers behind valuation changes—all without manual spreadsheet updates.

### **Relative Valuation with Benchmarking**

*Track fair value against market signals, not static models*

Automate portfolio company valuations with direct revenue updates and integrated benchmarks. AI-driven relative valuation models eliminate manual guesswork, ensuring investors see risk-adjusted performance instantly—trusted and secure for board-ready reporting.

### **Runway & Liquidity Outlook**

*Always know how much cash runway remains*

Dynamic integration with company financials delivers AI-powered forecasts of burn rate and liquidity. Automated alerts highlight when capital raises are needed, so investors can act decisively rather than reactively.

---

### **Growth Trend Analysis**

*Spot inflection points before quarterly reviews*

Automated data ingestion from ops, sales, and finance systems enables AI to surface revenue acceleration or slowdowns in real time. Investors gain early warnings of performance shifts, improving both oversight and portfolio strategy.

*Never miss a critical investor obligation*

Personas: Private Debt

## How is the portfolio company handling its obligations?

*I want to know how the company is paying its due or able to pay at any point in time.*

VM’s platform provide portfolio company insights to Investors at any point in time through it’s platform as well as automated monthly performance notifications.

### VM Debt Covenant Monitoring

- Can the company meet its debt obligations on time based on current performance and trajectory?
    - VM integrates borrower financials and banking data to present an up-to-date view of cash generation versus debt service. You get clear visibility into cash flow coverage, upcoming maturities, and scheduled payments, with trend lines that show whether current performance supports timely repayment or indicates emerging shortfalls.
- Which coverage ratios are trending toward thresholds, and what immediate actions should I consider?
    - Our platform continuously provide leverage, DSCR, interest coverage, and liquidity ratios derived from source data, displaying headroom to thresholds and how each metric is trending. Dashboards highlight the drivers behind tightening coverage (EBITDA movement, interest expense, cash buffers), enabling investors to quickly identify pressure points and consider targeted follow-ups with borrowers.
- Under base, downside, and stress scenarios, when could compliance or repayment risk emerge, and how much headroom do we have?
    - VM’s scenario analysis lets investors simulate base, downside, and stress cases to see how covenant ratios and cash coverage evolve over the next few quarters. The platform quantifies headroom by period and highlights when and where risks could surface, enabling lenders to plan well-informed responses, such as exploring amendments, waivers, or refinancing paths, based on a clear view of potential outcomes.

# V1

*Tagline: Continuous compliance insights — no surprises in your portfolio*

---

### **Automated Covenant Calculations & Compliance Tracking**

*Eliminate manual checks and accelerate reporting*

Our system integrates directly with journal and banking data to auto-calculate leverage, interest coverage, and liquidity ratios. Compliance snapshots update in real-time, streamlining monitoring and reducing reliance on borrower-provided spreadsheets.

---

### **Coverage Ratio Dashboards & Early Risk Alerts**

*Spot slippage before it turns into a breach*

AI tracks trends in EBITDA, debt service coverage, and liquidity buffers, sending proactive alerts when metrics drift near covenant thresholds. Investors can intervene early with borrowers, protecting downside and preserving deal value.

---

### **Scenario Stress-Testing of Borrower Covenants**

*Anticipate future breaches, not just past ones*

Our platform models covenant ratios under base, downside, and stress cases, projecting potential compliance risks months in advance. Backed by audit-grade data and expert oversight, it arms lenders with foresight to negotiate waivers or restructures proactively.

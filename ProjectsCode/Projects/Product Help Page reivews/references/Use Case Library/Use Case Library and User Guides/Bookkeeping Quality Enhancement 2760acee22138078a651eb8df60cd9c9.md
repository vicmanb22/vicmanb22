### **Error Detection & Anomaly Alerts**

*Spot issues before they become audit risks*

Machine learning scans transactions to flag duplicates, inconsistencies, or unusual movements in real time. CFOs and FP&A teams gain confidence that numbers reflect reality, not rework.

### **Audit-Ready Books on Demand**

*Compliance confidence without the crunch*

Automated reconciliations and standardized reporting formats ensure the books are clean and audit-ready year-round. Security-grade infrastructure safeguards sensitive financial data, giving founders and CFOs peace of mind.

### **Accelerated Close & Forecasting Inputs**

*From bookkeeping to board pack, faster than ever*

By eliminating manual clean-up, close cycles shorten dramatically. FP&A teams get reliable, timely inputs for forecasting, scenario modeling, and investor pack preparation—unlocking sharper insights with zero delays.

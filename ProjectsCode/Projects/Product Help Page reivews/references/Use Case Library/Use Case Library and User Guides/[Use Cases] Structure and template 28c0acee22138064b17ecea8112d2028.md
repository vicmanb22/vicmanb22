# [Use Cases] Structure and template

## V2 Structure:

### Fundamental question

- Hook to bring in the reader, something more interesting/ exciting/ casual to bring in the user to keep reading

### Terminal question

- An actual question they might ask
    - “Which business units are growing fastest?”
    - Terminal question might be different based on the audience, e.g. founders are generally less technical and financially literate, may ask broader questions like “where did all our money go” but FP&A guy may not ask that

### Answer:

- Part 1 - Describe a situation where they might actually ask this question
    - What is the problem they’re facing when asking this question?
    - What are they feeling they’re facing when asking this question?
    - What is the desired outcome?
- Part 2 - How does our product achieve their desired outcome?

Principle of how to make the use cases: Don’t write long bullshits that no one wants to read. Keep every questions and answers meaningful and short. 

### Overall Question

- Question that draws people’s attention, clients’ intrusive thoughts.
    - make sure this covers the main painpoints that we want to highlght.
- Two sentence answer
    - 1st sentence:
        - Crisis story that makes clients wants to read more
        - Something that keeps them up at night
    - 2nd sentence:
        - brief on what VM can help

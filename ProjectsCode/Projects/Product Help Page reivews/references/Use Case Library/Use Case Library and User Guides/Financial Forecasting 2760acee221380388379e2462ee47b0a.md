### **Cash Flow & Liquidity Forecasting**

*Anticipate funding needs and protect solvency*

By reconciling forecasted inflows and outflows against historical banking data, our platform builds forward-looking cash flow models. Investors and CFOs can instantly see liquidity runway, covenant headroom, and the timing of external capital needs.

Automated integrations ensure assumptions trace back to data, while our finance experts validate drivers for defensibility in diligence or board materials.

### **General**

**Website:** [https://www.23andme.com/](https://www.23andme.com/)

**Socials:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/23andme) [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/23andme) [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/23andMe) [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/23andme)

**Founded:** 2006

**Based In:** United States

**Industry:** Genetic Testing

**Type:** Public Company

**Description**

23andMe (NASDAQ:ME) offers DNA testing with the most comprehensive ancestry breakdown, personalized health insights, and more. Their services include Ancestry Service, Health plus Ancestry Service, 23andMe Premium, and 23andMe Total Health. These services provide insights into genetic predispositions, carrier status, wellness, and ancestry composition. 23andMe also offers advanced genetic testing techniques like exome sequencing and biannual blood testing, along with genetics-informed clinical care. The company emphasizes privacy and data protection, ensuring that customers' genetic information is secure and under their control.

---

### **Investor Summary**

**Business Model**

23andMe operates on a direct-to-consumer model, offering genetic testing kits that customers can purchase online. The company generates revenue through the sale of these kits and subscription services for ongoing health insights and updates. Additionally, 23andMe leverages its extensive genetic database for research collaborations and partnerships with pharmaceutical companies, providing another revenue stream.

**Growth Opportunities**

- Expansion into international markets to tap into a broader customer base.
- Development of new genetic testing services and health insights to stay ahead of technological advancements.
- Partnerships with healthcare providers to integrate genetic testing into routine medical care.
- Leveraging big data analytics to offer more personalized and actionable health recommendations.

**Biggest Risks**

- Regulatory changes that could impact the availability and scope of genetic testing services.
- Data privacy concerns that could erode customer trust and lead to decreased demand.
- Intense competition from established players and new entrants in the genetic testing market.
- Potential legal liabilities related to the accuracy and interpretation of genetic test results.

**Funding Recommendation**

23andMe should consider using retained earnings and bank financing to fund its growth initiatives. Given its established market position and revenue streams, these funding options can provide the necessary capital without diluting ownership. Additionally, exploring strategic partnerships and grants related to healthcare innovation could further support expansion and development efforts.

---

### **Competition**

**Landscape**

The genetic testing industry is highly competitive with key players like Ancestry.com, MyHeritage, and Helix offering similar ancestry and health insights. The market is driven by increasing consumer interest in personalized health and ancestry information, advancements in genetic testing technology, and growing awareness of genetic disorders. 23andMe's competitive edge lies in its comprehensive service offerings and strong emphasis on data privacy and security.

### **Diligence Focus Areas**

- **Revenue recognition:** Given the diverse range of services offered by 23andMe, it's crucial to understand how the company recognizes revenue from different streams (e.g., genetic testing kits, subscription services, clinical care). This ensures that revenue is recorded accurately and consistently.
- **Cost of goods sold (cogs):** Analyzing COGS helps in understanding the direct costs associated with producing genetic testing kits and other services. This is essential for assessing the company's gross margin and overall profitability.
- **Research and development (r&d) expenses:** As a company in the genetic testing industry, significant investments in R&D are likely. Evaluating R&D expenses helps in understanding the company's commitment to innovation and future growth potential.
- **Customer acquisition costs (cac):** Understanding how much the company spends to acquire new customers is critical in assessing the efficiency and sustainability of their growth strategy, especially in a competitive market.
- **Data privacy and compliance costs:** Given the sensitive nature of genetic data, it's important to evaluate the costs associated with ensuring data privacy and compliance with regulations like HIPAA. This impacts the company's risk profile and operational costs.
- **Deferred revenue:** Since 23andMe offers subscription services, deferred revenue is an important metric to examine. It indicates the amount of revenue that has been collected but not yet earned, impacting future revenue recognition.
- **Intellectual property (ip) valuation:** The company's value is significantly tied to its genetic testing technologies and databases. Assessing the value of IP can provide insights into the company's competitive advantage and potential for future revenue streams.
- **Customer refunds and returns:** Analyzing the rate and financial impact of customer refunds and returns can provide insights into customer satisfaction and product/service reliability, which are critical for long-term success.
- **Partnerships and collaborations:** Evaluating financial terms and conditions of partnerships with healthcare providers, research institutions, and other entities helps in understanding additional revenue streams and potential liabilities.

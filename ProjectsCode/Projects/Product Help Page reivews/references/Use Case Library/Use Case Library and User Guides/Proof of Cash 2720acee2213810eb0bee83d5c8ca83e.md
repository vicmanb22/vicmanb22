---

### **Automated Bank-to-Book Reconciliation**

*Instantly spot mismatches between statements and financial records*

Our platform connects directly to bank statements and accounting journals to reconcile ending balances automatically. AI flags timing gaps and misposts, ensuring investors gain assurance on the integrity and consistency of reported cash without manual tie-outs.

---

### **Cash Inflow Validation**

*Confirm that reported revenue truly made it to the bank*

AI-powered analysis matches journaled inflows with actual bank receipts to eliminate overstatement risks. This gives investors confidence that topline and collections reporting aligns with reality — critical for diligence on cash conversion cycles.

---

### **Cash Outflow Verification**

*Expose hidden leakage and validate spend discipline*

The system cross-checks outflow entries against bank transactions and counterparties, highlighting unusual disbursements or misclassified payments. By providing transparency into how cash leaves the business, investors gain assurance over expense legitimacy and management control.

---

### **Counterparty Confirmation**

*Ensure every transaction is booked to the right party*

Our engine not only matches values but also validates counterparties, surfacing discrepancies between banking records and journal entries. This prevents misallocations and strengthens trust in vendor, customer, or related-party representations.

---

### **Liquidity Stress-Test & Short-Term Solvency Check**

*Ensure the business can meet obligations post-close*

Automatic integration with ERP data enables real-time liquidity testing, measuring coverage of payables, wages, and supplier obligations. AI-driven ratio analysis (current & quick ratios) gives investors fast insight into solvency risks and cushions needed to maintain business continuity.

---

### **Cash Conversion Cycle & Operational Efficiency Review**

*Pinpoint trapped cash across receivables, payables, and inventory*

Our platform benchmarks Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), Days Payable Outstanding (DPO), and Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO) against industry peers. Investors can quickly identify inefficiencies in collections or procurement, quantifying immediate levers to unlock free cash flow.

---

### **Normalized Working Capital & Deal Peg**

*Strip out one-offs and set a fair baseline for negotiations*

We automatically adjust for non-recurring items, debt-like liabilities, and seasonal swings to calculate a normalized working capital requirement. This provides a defensible “peg” for purchase agreement negotiations — ensuring investors don’t overpay for inflated balance sheets.

---

### **Liquidity Forecasting for Value Creation**

*Project post-deal cash needs with precision*

AI-assisted modeling links working capital dynamics to revenue forecasts, highlighting whether growth plans are self-fundable or require fresh capital. This equips private equity and debt investors with a sharper lens on exit readiness, IRR timing, and leverage sustainability.

### **Revenue Concentration & Exposure Alerts**

*Know when your top customers become a hidden liability*

Automated data integration from CRM and financial systems flags when revenue is overly concentrated in a handful of clients. AI-driven scenario analysis helps CFOs model churn risk and prepare diversification strategies, ensuring sustainable growth and investor confidence.

---

### **Runway & Leverage Risk Forecasting**

*Stay ahead of liquidity crunches and debt exposures*

AI-powered forecasting integrates burn rate, debt schedules, and leverage ratios in real time. Automated dashboards highlight when cash runway dips below a safe zone or when covenants approach breach, enabling proactive financing or expense adjustments.

---

### **Expense & Compliance Risk Controls**

*Eliminate surprises in spend and operations*

Direct data integration across expense systems, payroll, and vendor flows helps identify hidden inefficiencies and compliance gaps. Anomaly detection flags unusual cost spikes or missing filings, giving CFOs the ability to course-correct before they become critical.

**Overall Risk Level: 🟡 MODERATE**

**Key Findings:**

- **Industry diversification is strong** across 12+ verticals
- **High-engagement customers drive majority of revenue** (positive)
- **Significant portion of revenue from transactional customers** (concern)

### **🚀 Strategic Recommendations**

**1. Immediate Actions (0-30 days):**

- **Customer Success Outreach**: Engage declining customers (BluePeak, NorthStar, GreenLeaf)
- **Churn Prevention**: Immediate outreach to inactive accounts ($500K at risk)
- **Contract Reviews**: Analyze why high-value customers show completion patterns

**2. Medium-term Strategy (30-90 days):**

- **Subscription Model Enhancement**: Convert project-based customers to recurring revenue
- **Engagement Scoring**: Implement customer health scoring based on transaction frequency
- **Expansion Revenue**: Focus on growing existing high-engagement customers

**3. Long-term Optimization (90+ days):**

- **Revenue Mix Target**: 70% recurring, 30% project-based
- **Customer Success Program**: Formal program for accounts >$100K ARR
- **New Customer Onboarding**: Improve initial engagement to reduce minimal-engagement segment

**The revenue concentration risk is well-managed, but the business model shows characteristics of professional services rather than pure SaaS. Focus should be on increasing recurring revenue components and improving customer engagement consistency.**

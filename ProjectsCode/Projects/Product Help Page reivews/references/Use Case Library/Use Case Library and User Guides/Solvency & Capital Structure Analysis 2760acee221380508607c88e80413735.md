### **Automated Leverage & Debt-Capacity Assessment**

*Instantly see if the capital stack can sustain operations and growth*

Our platform integrates directly with the target’s ledgers and loan schedules to calculate debt-to-equity ratios, leverage trends, and serviceability metrics. AI highlights over-leverage or excess capacity, giving investors a transparent view of balance sheet resilience.

---

### **Coverage Ratios & Covenant Risk Alerts**

*Catch potential breaches before they erode deal value*

We automatically compute interest and fixed-charge coverage ratios, benchmarking them against market standards. AI flags stress points and covenant headroom issues, enabling investors to pressure-test whether earnings can cover obligations under multiple scenarios.

---

### **Capital Structure Optimization Scenarios**

*Map refinancing, deleveraging, and liquidity options in minutes*

Scenario modeling evaluates alternative debt/equity structures, highlighting refinancing costs and equity dilution risks. Built-in AI stress tests simulate downturns, ensuring investors see not only today’s solvency but also future flexibility in managing debt obligations.

---

### **Hidden Liabilities & Off-Balance Sheet Exposure Check**

*No surprises post-close*

The system automatically identifies debt-like obligations, including leases, guarantees, and contingent liabilities often buried in footnotes or disclosures. Audit-trained experts validate AI findings, ensuring investors have a complete picture of the true enterprise obligations.

### Balance Sheet Resilience Assessment

**✅ Strengths:**

- **Strong asset coverage:** 10.75x ratio provides substantial debt backing
- **Excellent interest coverage:** 42.55x LTM coverage shows strong serviceability
- **Low debt-to-assets:** 9.3% indicates conservative debt usage
- **Profitable operations:** Positive EBITDA in 8 of 12 LTM months

**⚠️ Areas of Concern:**

- **High short-term debt concentration:** 84% maturing within 1 year
- **Credit card dependency:** $193K in high-cost revolving debt
- **Seasonal coverage stress:** Negative coverage during loss months
- **Rising liability base:** 2025-Q2 shows increased leverage

**🔍 Critical Risk Factors:**

- **Refinancing risk:** $446K short-term debt requires renewal/repayment
- **Interest rate sensitivity:** Variable rate exposure on credit facilities
- **Cash flow dependency:** Debt service tied to operational performance
- **Working capital pressure:** Current liabilities exceed long-term debt

### Strategic Recommendations

**🎯 Immediate Actions:**

1. **Debt restructuring:** Convert $193K credit card debt to term loans at lower rates
2. **Credit facility optimization:** Negotiate revolving credit line for seasonal cash needs
3. **Principal prepayments:** Use strong cash months to reduce short-term debt

**📊 Long-term Structure:**

- **Target D/E ratio:** Reduce from 1.21x to 0.8x through retained earnings
- **Maturity extension:** Convert 50% of short-term debt to 2-3 year terms
- **Cost optimization:** Replace high-cost credit cards with bank facilities

**📈 Monitoring Metrics:**

- **Monthly DSCR:** Maintain >2.0x during profitable months
- **Cash runway:** Ensure 6+ months coverage during seasonal downturns
- **Interest coverage:** Target >10x minimum during weak quarters

## Overall Leverage Assessment

MedA demonstrates **moderate leverage with strong serviceability** during profitable periods, but faces **refinancing risk** due to high short-term debt concentration. The company's **excellent asset coverage** and **strong interest coverage** provide resilience, but **seasonal cash flow volatility** creates periodic stress on debt service capability. Priority should be **debt restructuring** to extend maturity and reduce refinancing risk.

What valuation methods would VM incorporate?

We use only valuation methods widely accepted in the industry, strictly adhering to IPEV valuation principles. This approach ensures your valuations are consistent, credible, and comparable—essential for investor reporting and regulatory compliance.

Our platform automatically selects and weights the most appropriate methods based on each company's characteristics. For high-growth SaaS companies, we emphasize revenue multiples; for mature businesses, we lean on DCF analysis. Every approach aligns with industry standards and best practices.
